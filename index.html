We’re Rinjani Game Studio, an independent team from Indonesia. We make games for the thrill of figuring it out, the laugh when it all goes sideways, and the urge to try again.

Our starting point is simple: is it fun to play? Then we keep building, testing, and tweaking until the answer is yes.
